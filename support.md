# Out of Office: Support

Out of Office has six logic puzzles with Easy, Medium and Hard levels. Each game explains its rules behind the question mark at the top of its screen.

## Common questions

**Where is my progress saved?**
On your iPhone only. Nothing is sent anywhere, so there's no account to sign in to.

**I got a new iPhone. Will my progress come with me?**
Not in this version. Progress stays on the iPhone where you played.

**How do I start over?**
Open Settings (the gear) and choose Reset progress. This erases your solved puzzles, times and streaks.

**A puzzle seems too hard. Can I skip it?**
Yes, tap Skip. Skipped puzzles come back later.

**How do hints work?**
Tap the lightbulb. A hint first points out a mistake, then the next logical step and why it works, then fills it in. The free version has 2 hints a day.

**What is a coffee break?**
After each game's first 10 puzzles, the free version takes a 30-second break after every 3 solved puzzles. You can always carry on when the countdown ends.

**What is No More Breaks?**
A one-time purchase: no coffee breaks and unlimited hints in every game, forever. Buy it in Settings > No More Breaks.

**I bought No More Breaks. How do I get it on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?**
Open Settings > No More Breaks and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Does the app need the internet?**
No. It works anywhere, including in flight mode. Only buying or restoring No More Breaks needs a connection.
