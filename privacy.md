# Out of Office: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Out of Office is a puzzle app that works entirely on your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your puzzle progress, solve times, streaks and settings are saved on your iPhone only, so you can carry on where you left off. They never leave the device. You can erase them at any time in Settings > Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
